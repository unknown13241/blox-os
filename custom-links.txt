HOW TO GET A PUBLIC LINK
-----------------------------------------
1) Cloudflare Tunnel (free):
   cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:3000
   Paste the https://....trycloudflare.com URL into
   Settings → Share links → Public URL.

2) ngrok (free):
   ngrok http 3000

3) Custom domain on your own server:
   Point a DNS A/AAAA record at your machine, run a reverse
   proxy (nginx/Caddy) to port 3000, then add the domain in
   Settings → Share links.

HOW TO USE http://blox.os:3000
--------------------------------
Add this line to C:\Windows\System32\drivers\etc\hosts
(open Notepad as Administrator):

127.0.0.1   blox.os

Then open http://blox.os:3000 in your browser.
